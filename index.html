FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

When should we book the spring opening clean?

The last two weeks of April fill first. Cottages sealed from Thanksgiving onward hold damp and must in the runs all winter, and owners booking from Toronto or London in March get the opening-weekend slot; after that we are working around your guest calendar. A key arrangement at the village hardware store has solved harder problems.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified, trained, and insured, every visit, and documented as such. Ontario runs no certification program for this trade, so those credentials are precisely what cottage and homeowners here should insist on before anyone touches the system.

How often should a cottage or Bayfield home clean its ducts?

A seasonal cottage does well on a two to three year cycle booked at spring opening, while year-round shore homes with pets, renovations, or busy rental calendars often need it sooner. If opening weekend smells like a cellar, the answer is now.

How do I verify the work on a property I rarely visit?

Every job ends with camera photos of the duct interiors before and after, plus written notes, sent to you wherever you are. Remote owners judge our work from evidence, not from faith in a voicemail.

Can you get the musty smell out before guests arrive?

Yes, in the right order. Cleaning removes the damp residue carrying the odour, then a sanitizer treatment on the clean ducts handles the rest. Skipping the cleaning step just perfumes the problem, so we never skip it.

Do you work beyond the village itself?

Yes, along the shore and inland across Huron County, including Goderich, Exeter, Clinton, Grand Bend, Zurich, and Seaforth, with access arrangements for offsite owners.

Why is our shoreline house dusty within a day of cleaning?

Usually a leaking return duct drawing from a damp crawl space or unfinished basement and serving it to every register. On the shore the fix is the same as inland, find the leaks with the system under gauge and seal them, and the film stops coming back between your spring opening and the Thanksgiving close-up.

What does a cleaning involve and how long does it take?

Vacuum onto the trunk lines, registers off and washed, each branch brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, photos before and after. A cottage typically takes two to three hours, a larger year-round home more.

Do you serve areas outside of Bayfield, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Bayfield and nearby communities including Goderich, Exeter, Clinton, Grand Bend, and the surrounding area.
